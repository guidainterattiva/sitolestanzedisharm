La Stanza di Valentina

Private terrace with a Moorish arch overlooking the Red Sea, marble bathroom.

Apartment with sea-view terrace · Domina Coral Bay, Sharm el Sheikh

  • 1 bedroom
  • 1 bathroom
  • Sea view

La Stanza di Valentina is a fully renovated apartment with a private terrace framed by a Moorish arch, overlooking the garden and the Red Sea: the perfect spot for breakfast and sunset drinks.

Inside, it's a boutique-hotel experience with the freedom of an apartment: a Calacatta marble bathroom with walk-in shower, made-to-measure wardrobes with mirrored doors, ambient LED lighting, a TV and a white kitchenette with kettle and coffee machine.

It's made for romantic getaways, for remote working with a sea view and for longer stays, with everything Coral Bay has to offer just steps away.
