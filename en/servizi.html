Airport transfer

A driver meets you at Sharm el Sheikh airport, takes you straight to your room and drives you back to the airport when you leave. It's a journey of about 8 km: a quarter of an hour.

Golf buggies and scooters

Coral Bay is big: to get around between the beach, pools and restaurants you can hire a 4-seater golf buggy or a scooter. You must be of legal age.
