La Stanza di Gabriele

45 m² on the ground floor, with sea views, a short walk from the Coral Bay beach.

One-bedroom apartment · Domina Coral Bay, Sharm el Sheikh

  • up to 4 guests
  • 45 m²
  • 1 bedroom
  • 1 bathroom
  • Sea view
  • Ground floor

La Stanza di Gabriele is a 45 m² ground-floor apartment in the Sheikh Coast area of the Domina Coral Bay: point 34 on the resort map, a short walk from the sea. It has a double bedroom, a fully equipped kitchenette and a bathroom with shower, and sleeps up to 4 people.

The Moorish arch in the hallway, the sky-blue walls and the Sharm light set the tone: simple, clean and comfortable. The kitchen has an induction hob, fridge, microwave, kettle, coffee machine and washing machine: handy for longer stays.

From the apartment you can walk to the resort's beach and pools; the main reception, pharmacy and minimarket are all inside Coral Bay. For everything else there's Massimiliano, Italian-speaking, on WhatsApp.
