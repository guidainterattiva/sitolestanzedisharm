Where we are

Domina Coral Bay

Our rooms are inside the Domina Coral Bay, one of the largest and most complete resorts in Sharm el Sheikh, on the bay of Marsa El Dekhela: a small seaside village with beaches, pools, restaurants, shops and services.

Our location

Point 34, Sheikh Coast

We're at point 34 on the resort map, in the Sheikh Coast area, close to the sea.

At the resort entrance, staff check your booking and documents: keep them to hand when you arrive.

Included in your stay

Beach, pools and resort

The resort's restaurants, bars, spa, tennis and diving centre are at an extra charge: you'll find up-to-date prices at reception.

Beach with sunloungers

Access to the resort beach, including the Main Beach and the floating pool.

Pools

Coral Bay's pools are open to guests.

Entertainment and kids' club

Entertainment and a kids' club, following the resort's programme.

Parking

Parking area inside the resort, included.

Resort services

Everything close at hand

Main reception

At the Main Gate, open 24 hours a day: this is where you check in and out and collect your keys.

Minimarket and pharmacy

Inside the resort, in the Piazzetta area.

Cash machine

ATM inside the central reception.

Medical centre

On site in the resort, for any medical needs during your holiday.
